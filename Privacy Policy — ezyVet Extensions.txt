# Privacy Policy – ezyVet Notes Alert

**Last updated:** 21 October 2026

## 1. What the Extension Does

ezyVet Notes Alert is an independent browser extension designed to display important contact and animal notes when opening records in ezyVet. Its purpose is to help veterinary practice staff notice important information.

## 2. Information and Privacy

The extension reads relevant contact and animal notes from the ezyVet page currently open in the browser. This information is processed locally in the browser to display the alert.

The extension does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or transmit personal information to external servers. It does not send notes or patient information to the developer.

Users should continue to follow their practice's privacy, confidentiality, and data-protection policies when using the extension.

## 3. Independent Software

ezyVet Notes Alert is an independent, third-party extension. It is not created, owned, endorsed, supported, or operated by ezyVet or its parent company. ezyVet is a separate product and trademark of its respective owner.

## 4. Use at Your Own Risk

The extension is provided free of charge and on an “as is” basis, without guarantees that it will always work correctly or display every important note.

The extension is intended as an additional aid only. It does not replace checking patient records, following clinic procedures, or exercising professional judgement. Users remain responsible for verifying important information in ezyVet.

Use of the extension is at the user's own risk. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer accepts no liability for losses, errors, missed alerts, or other consequences arising from its use or inability to function.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the extension changes. The latest version will be made available alongside the extension.

## 6. Contact

For questions about this extension or this privacy policy, please contact the extension developer through the contact details provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
