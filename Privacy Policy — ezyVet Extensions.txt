Privacy Policy — ezyVet Extensions
Last updated: 12 October 2026
1. Overview
The ezyVet Notes Alert and ezyVet Financial Comments Chrome extensions are designed to assist authorised veterinary hospital staff when using the ezyVet practice management system.
These extensions help staff access relevant client, patient and financial information within the ezyVet interface.
2. Information Accessed
The extensions may access information displayed within the ezyVet system, including:
* Client contact notes.
* Animal or patient notes.
* Notes marked as important.
* Existing client comments displayed in the financial section, where applicable.
The extensions only access information necessary to provide their intended functionality.
3. Collection and Sharing of Information
The extensions do not independently collect, sell, share or transmit personal information to external parties.
Information displayed by the extensions remains within the ezyVet interface and the user's browser. The extensions do not send information to third-party analytics services or advertising networks.
4. Data Storage
The extensions do not intentionally store client, patient or financial information outside the ezyVet system.
Any information displayed by the extensions is obtained from the ezyVet interface and is not intended to create a separate database of client or patient information.
5. Permissions
The extensions require permission to operate on the hospital's ezyVet website. This access is necessary to identify the relevant record information and display it in the intended location.
The extensions are not intended to access unrelated websites or browser activity.
6. Information Security
The extensions are intended for use by authorised hospital staff as part of their normal ezyVet workflow. Users must continue to follow the hospital's confidentiality, access-control and information-security procedures.
The extensions do not replace the security controls provided by ezyVet or the hospital.
7. Changes to This Policy
This privacy policy may be updated if the extensions' functionality or data practices change. The updated policy will be made available alongside the extension listing where appropriate.
8. Contact
For questions about these extensions or this privacy policy, please contact the hospital's designated extension maintainer.
